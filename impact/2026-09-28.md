# Accomplishments: Week of September 28, 2026

## Vault Enterprise PR's

- [PR #19091](https://github.com/hashicorp/vault-enterprise/pull/19091): Fix database plugin callback cleanup when plugin version reporting is optional. Resolved the `VAULT-50722` escalation's unbounded resource growth by ensuring setup-created callback servers are stopped during cleanup.

## Vault Documentation PR's
