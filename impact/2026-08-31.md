### JIRA

- [VAULT-50116](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-50116): Root-namespace `vault plugin reload -mounts` routed to `/sys/plugins/reload/unknown`. Created and triaged the issue, documented the API workaround, and moved it to In Review.

- [VAULT-49618](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-49618) (cont.): Vault UI namespace-login flickered and mishandled slash entry. Investigated and closed the issue after the fix and release backports were prepared.

- [VAULT-49661](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-49661) (cont.): `vault recover -snapshot-id` panicked without a path argument. Validated the fix and targeted Vault `1.21.11`, `2.1.1`, and `2.2.0`.

- [VAULT-49773](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-49773): SCIM `unlink-entity` exposed a misleading alias-consent contract. Triaged the behavior and linked [PR #18237](https://github.com/hashicorp/vault-enterprise/pull/18237), which clarifies alias roles and response fields.

- [VAULT-49783](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-49783): PKI CMPv2 requests could trigger a nil-map panic when sent to the wrong node. Reproduced the issue and confirmed active-node requirements and target fixes.

- [VAULT-49731](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-49731): Transit CSR re-signing dropped X.509 extension criticality and could emit malformed ASN.1. Identified stale request attributes and duplicate SAN serialization as separate causes.

- [VAULT-48835](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-48835), [VAULT-48837](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-48837), and [VAULT-48838](https://hashicorp.atlassian.net/browse/VAULT-48838) (cont.): Continued AI-assisted Customer Engineering workflow development and documented practical workflow variations.
